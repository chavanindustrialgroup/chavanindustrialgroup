01 The Beginning

Shubhankar Chavan is a young entrepreneur from Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra, who founded Chavan Industrial Group (CIG) with the belief that institutional supply — to organisations like Indian Railways — is not the same as selling a product. It is a discipline that rewards precision, patience, and consistency.

In just over two years, CIG has grown from a solo effort into a team of five, sourcing, manufacturing and delivering high-quality materials and components to demanding institutional clients. The work spans communication equipment, surveillance systems, fire-safety gear, and industrial supply — each category representing a different technical specification and documentation standard.

The company operates as a registered MSME, with a growing track record of institutional orders executed on time, to specification, and with full documentation.

02 The Approach

Institutional supply — to an organisation such as Indian Railways — is a chain of disciplines: reading specifications precisely, sourcing to standard, preparing documentation, coordinating inspection, managing logistics, and meeting delivery commitments.

Shubhankar has approached this work the way it is best approached: without noise, and without skipping steps. Each order is treated not just as revenue, but as an asset in credibility — the kind of credibility that larger contracts are built upon.

Technical specifications must be understood. Procurement procedures must be followed. Compliance must be exact. Each order trains the next, which is why the categories keep widening and the company’s position as a sourcing and supply partner keeps deepening.

“You don’t need to begin with a massive company. You need to begin with an opportunity, deliver when it arrives, and build credibility one assignment at a time.”

Shubhankar Chavan
